About

From data warehouses to agent graphs

I'm an AI/ML Engineer based in Mumbai, currently completing a Master's in Computer Applications specialized in AI/ML. Over the last 3.8+ years I've worked across the full ML lifecycle, from cleaning transactional retail data at Fortune 500 scale to designing multi-agent systems that route, retrieve, and respond.

At TCS, I built computer vision models to automate visual quality inspection, trained demand-forecasting models (ARIMA, LSTM, Prophet), and shipped everything through Docker and FastAPI with Prometheus-based drift monitoring that caught model degradation before it became a business problem.

Lately my focus has shifted toward agentic AI: LangGraph orchestration, FAISS-backed RAG, and the guardrails needed to make LLM systems safe to deploy: intent classification, PII redaction, and rule-based routing across specialist agents.
